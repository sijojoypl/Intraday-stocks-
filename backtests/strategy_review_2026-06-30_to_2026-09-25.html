Breakout Strategy Review

Three months of real 1-minute Dhan data, replayed through the bot's own backtest engine, then tuned with an in-sample / out-of-sample split.

Period: 30 Jun – 25 Sep 2026 (63 trading days)Sizing: ₹1,00,000 per tradeCosts: Angel One intradayBranch: claude/laughing-gates-hp2maa
Recommendation Keep the live settings. Paper-test a 12:00 entry cutoff.
  • With current settings the strategy broke even over 3 months: ₹6,194 gross profit, ₹6,224 charges, −₹30 net.
  • Every setting picked on the first two months lost money on the last month. No tuned setting held up out of sample.
  • Stopping new entries at 12:00 beat the live 15:00 cutoff in every month (+₹5,135 vs −₹30), but it still lost in September. Run it on paper next to the live bot before changing entry_cutoff.

Stock selection

Daily picks

Nifty gap = official open vs previous close (±0.2% threshold). Gap-up → short the top 2 gainers. Gap-down → buy the bottom 2. Flat → short the top 1 and buy the bottom 1.

How this was built

Method and data

  • Data. Dhan official daily candles for all 50 Nifty stocks (gap ranking) and full 1-minute bars 09:15–15:14 for the 2 picked stocks each day: 126 stock-days × 360 bars, none missing, built into 120 three-minute candles per pick.
  • Engine. The repo's own BacktestEngine: 09:15–09:18 reference candle, entry on a 3-minute close beyond it, stop at the other side of the candle (or 1% if the candle is wider than 1%), 1% target, stop checked first when both hit in one candle, square-off at 15:15.
  • Costs. Angel One intraday charges per trade, from src/analysis/transaction_costs.py; quantity = ₹1,00,000 ÷ entry price.
  • Split. 304 settings with ≥20 training trades were ranked on 30 Jun – 26 Aug (42 days) and scored on 27 Aug – 25 Sep (21 days).
  • Checks. Every reference candle opens at Dhan's official daily open. A fresh re-run reproduced the baseline to the rupee.

Limits

  • Three months and 75 baseline trades is a small sample; a 21-day test period can't separate a small real improvement from noise.
  • Dhan's 1-minute data ends at 15:14, so 15:15 square-offs use the 15:14 close.
  • The stock universe is today's config/nifty50.json, not the index as it stood each day.
  • Intraday Nifty wasn't collected, so the Nifty-confirmation filter couldn't be tested.
  • No slippage beyond charges is modelled; stop and target fills are assumed exact.
