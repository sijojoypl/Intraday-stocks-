# Strategy optimisation: 30 Jun – 25 Sep 2026

Data: Dhan 1-min bars for both picked stocks on every trading day (126 stock-days, 360 bars each, 0 missing), built into 3-min candles (120 per pick) in `backtests/dhan_collection/days/` (63 day files).
Capital ₹1,00,000 per trade, Angel One costs. All numbers are net of costs.

Note: the data has no intraday Nifty series, so every `nifty_confirm` setting produces no trades. Those settings are ignored here.

- Train: 30 Jun – 26 Aug (42 days)
- Test: 27 Aug – 25 Sep (21 days)

### Settings that made money on both train and test

All 12 are **short-only** variants (fade/short, cutoff 12:00 or 15:00). The best of them:

| Setting | Train | Test | Jul | Aug | Sep |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| fade/short, cutoff 12:00, target 2R | 32t ₹6,553 | 9t ₹905 | ₹-239 | ₹6,940 | ₹905 |
| fade/short, cutoff 15:00, target 2R | 34t ₹6,463 | 10t ₹436 | ₹-255 | ₹6,866 | ₹436 |

None of these is convincing. The test profit is ₹200–900 on 9–10 trades, which is one winning trade's worth. They lost money in July, and almost all their profit came from August. That is a one-off, not an edge.

## Recommendation

**No change to `config/settings.json` is justified by this data.**

- Every setting ranked by the training period lost money in the test period. The only settings that were positive on test are short-only variants with 9–10 test trades, a losing July, and one strong August.
- Three months and 75 baseline trades is a small sample, and a test period of 21 days with 10–21 trades can't separate a real improvement from noise.
- The one candidate worth watching is an **earlier entry cutoff (12:00)**. It is the only change that improved every month, including the test month, and it has a simple story: late breakouts have too little time to reach the target and mostly end in the 15:15 square-off. But it still lost money out of sample. It should be forward-tested (paper or logged alongside live) and re-checked when more months of data are available, before anyone changes the live setting.

Reproduce:

```
python scripts/backtest_range.py --start 2026-06-30 --end 2026-09-25 --source csv --data-dir backtests/dhan_collection/days
python scripts/optimize_strategy.py --start 2026-06-30 --end 2026-09-25 --source csv --data-dir backtests/dhan_collection/days --min-trades 20 --top 15
```
