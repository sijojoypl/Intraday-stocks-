Strategy optimisation: 30 Jun – 25 Sep 2026

63 trading days · 2 picks per day · ₹1,00,000 per trade · Angel One costs · all figures net of costs

Recommendation
Keep the live settings. No change is justified by this data.
  • Every setting ranked on the training period lost money on the test period.
  • The only settings that were positive on test are short-only, with 9–10 test trades, a losing July and one strong August.
  • One candidate to watch: an earlier entry cutoff at 12:00. It was the only change that beat the live settings in all three months. It still lost money on the test period, so forward-test it before touching config/settings.json.

The 13 trades entered after 12:00 lost about ₹5,165 in total. Late breakouts have little time to reach the target and mostly end in the 15:15 square-off. The 12:00 cutoff beat 15:00 in every month, but it still lost money in September and on the test period (14 trades, ₹-2,905).

Why no change

Three months and 75 baseline trades is a small sample. A 21-day test period with 10–21 trades can't separate a real improvement from noise. The next useful step is more history (for example March to June 2026) and a rerun of the same split, to see whether the 12:00 cutoff holds up.

The data has no intraday Nifty series, so every nifty_confirm setting produced no trades. Those settings are left out.

Data and how to reproduce

Dhan 1-minute bars for both picked stocks on every trading day: 126 stock-days, 360 bars each, none missing. Built into 3-minute candles (120 per pick) in backtests/dhan_collection/days/.

python scripts/backtest_range.py --start 2026-06-30 --end 2026-09-25 \
  --source csv --data-dir backtests/dhan_collection/days

python scripts/optimize_strategy.py --start 2026-06-30 --end 2026-09-25 \
  --source csv --data-dir backtests/dhan_collection/days --min-trades 20 --top 15
Backtest only. Past results on 63 days don't predict live performance.
